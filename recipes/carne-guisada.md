# Carne Guisada

Mexican-style braised beef stew. The beef goes in cubed and comes out fork-tender.

| Prep | Cook | Total | Yield |
| ---- | ---- | ----- | ----- |
| 10 min | 30 min | 40 min | 3–4 servings |

## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient | Kitchen status |
| ------ | ---------- | -------------- |
| 1 Tbsp | Oil | ✅ Colavita EVOO |
| 1 lb | Cubed beef stew meat | 🛒 Buy |
| 1 Tbsp | Flour | ✅ All-purpose flour |
| 1 Tbsp | Tomato paste | 🛒 Buy |
| ½ | Onion, diced | 🛒 Buy |
| ½ | Green bell pepper, diced | 🛒 Buy |
| 1 small | Jalapeño, finely diced — seeds removed if you want it less spicy | 🛒 Buy |
| 1 | Tomato, diced | 🛒 Buy |
| 4 cloves | Garlic, diced | 🛒 Buy |
| ½ tsp | Chipotle chili powder | ✅ Sub: Dunya chili powder + a spoon of Quesada chipotle hot sauce |
| ½ tsp | Paprika | ✅ Have |
| ¼ tsp | Black pepper | ✅ Venetian Gold ground black pepper |
| ½ tsp | Salt | ⚠️ Only the unlabeled white jar — taste before using |
| ¼ tsp | Cumin | 🛒 Buy |
| ¼ tsp | Coriander | 🛒 Buy |
| ½ tsp | Oregano | ✅ Supreme Tradition oregano leaves |
| 1 cup | Beef stock | 🛒 Buy |
| ¼ cup | Fresh cilantro, finely chopped | 🛒 Buy |
| ½ cup | Shredded chihuahua or mozzarella cheese | 🛒 Buy |
| to serve | Tortillas, or rice | 🛒 Buy |

## Instructions

1. **Sear the beef.** Heat a large skillet over medium-high and add the oil and cubed beef. Sear about 2–3 minutes on all sides until golden brown. Sprinkle the flour over the beef, add the tomato paste, and stir to coat the beef evenly. Reduce heat to medium.

2. **Soften the vegetables.** Add the diced onion, bell pepper, jalapeño, tomato and garlic. Cook, stirring, for about 3–4 minutes until the onions and peppers have softened. Add the chipotle chili powder, paprika, black pepper, salt, cumin, coriander and oregano, and stir to combine.

3. **Simmer.** Add the beef stock, bring to a simmer, then reduce the heat to medium-low and cover with a tight-fitting lid. Cook 20–30 minutes until the beef is very tender and shreds easily with a fork, and the liquid has reduced.

4. **Serve.** Remove from the heat and top with the shredded cheese and fresh chopped cilantro. Serve with tortillas for dipping, or spoon over rice.

## Notes

- **Don't rush step 3.** If the beef isn't fork-shreddable at 30 minutes, add a splash more stock and keep going. Stew meat forgives long cooking; it does not forgive short cooking.
- **No chipotle chili powder?** Regular chili powder plus a spoonful of the Quesada chipotle hot sauce gets you the same smoky heat.
- **Ground beef is not a substitute.** It won't shred — the cubed stew meat is what makes the dish.
