taken on an iPhone. The files listed below are **not** — they are freely-licensed
stand-ins from Wikimedia Commons, used for ingredients that were never
photographed. They show the right ingredient, not the specific product in the
kitchen, so swap any of them for a real photo whenever you take one (drop the
photo in `images/` under the same filename and delete the line here).
